## Primary-source checks

### 1. NCTB's syllabus and marks-distribution index

Official page: https://nctb.gov.bd/site/page/9a90c854-ce5f-4d77-9915-1ecd0953079d/SSC-&-HSC-Short-Syllabus

A live Firecrawl retrieval on 14 November 2026 returned the NCTB page content and link set. The page's displayed update date is 23 June 2025. Its HSC-specific list includes:
- 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025 HSC examination syllabus entries (rearranged syllabuses).
- An entry for the **2024–25 XI–XII session / HSC 2026** covering Bangla First Paper syllabus and English First Paper marks distribution.

This index entry is explicitly for HSC 2026, not HSC 2027. It cannot be reused as HSC 2027 evidence.

### 2. NCTB notice index and the 2026–27 syllabus

Official notice index: https://nctb.gov.bd/pages/notices/

The index identifies a notice dated **1 October 2026** titled “২০২৬-২৭ শিক্ষাবর্ষের উচ্চ মাধ্যমিক ও সমমান শ্রেণির জন্য নির্ধারিত পাঠ্যসূচি” (prescribed syllabus for higher secondary and equivalent classes for academic year 2026–27). This is the next cohort's source family and must remain in the **HSC 2028** bucket unless NCTB explicitly states otherwise.

The HSC 2028 source register separately records official-hosted NCTB PDFs for ICT, Bangla First Paper and English First Paper. Those documents are not HSC 2027 documents.

### 3. HSC 2027 Bangla First Paper notice lead

The prior tranche located an official NCTB notice dated **26 November 2025** concerning HSC 2027 Bangla First Paper. The notice page was found, but the official “File 1” attachment URL was not exposed by the available page extraction. The attachment has not been read. The related 21 November 2024 NCTB circular confirms that the 2024–25 syllabus attachment existed and that the syllabus included twelve stories and twelve poems, but that circular alone does not verify a 2025–26 carry-forward or the complete HSC 2027 reading list.

Keep the HSC 2027 carry-forward claim secondary-reported until the 26 November 2025 attachment itself is retrieved and checked.

## Secondary discovery lead — not an official board syllabus

A school-hosted PDF was discovered:
https://cesc.edu.bd/public/ckfinder/userfiles/files/Syllabus_XI_%26_XII_(Science)_HSC%202027.pdf

The title identifies Cantonment English School & College and its 2025–2026 XI–XII Science session. Search extraction exposes school examination schedules and test-exam/term-exam marks tables for multiple subjects. This is a school-level academic plan, not an NCTB or education-board HSC 2027 subject syllabus. It may be retained as a clearly labelled local school planning example, but must not be used to assert board syllabus coverage, national marks distribution or predicted question weightings.
